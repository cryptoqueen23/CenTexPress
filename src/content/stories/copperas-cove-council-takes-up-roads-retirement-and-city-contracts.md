# Copperas Cove Council Takes Up Roads, Retirement and City Contracts

**COPPERAS COVE, Texas —** The Copperas Cove City Council took up several spending and policy items during its September 15 meeting, including roadway improvements, employee retirement contributions, a school policing agreement and construction of an athletic storage building.

Among the items before the council was a professional services agreement connected to improvements along **Veterans Avenue and West Avenue D**.

Council also considered an agreement with the **Copperas Cove Independent School District for policing services**, as well as a contract with **Seneca Construction Management, LLC** for an athletic storage building.

Another item addressed the city's contribution rate to the **Texas Municipal Retirement System (TMRS)**, which provides retirement benefits for participating municipal employees.

The meeting also included a public hearing concerning a conditional-use permit for a short-term rental at **1242 Lexington Drive**.

These items matter because decisions made through council contracts, employee-benefit commitments and capital projects can create costs extending beyond a single council meeting or budget year.

CenTex Press will continue reviewing the underlying agreements, costs and supporting documents associated with these decisions.
